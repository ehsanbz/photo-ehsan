# Preview photographs

These four images are generated **sample assets**, not photographs taken by Ehsan. They demonstrate the layout and are isolated in this folder for replacement. They were generated with the built-in image tool, then exported as WebP at quality 82 and at most 2400 px on the long edge. Their source prompts were:

- `berlin-train.webp`: Documentary photograph of an elevated Berlin S-Bahn crossing a layered cityscape at blue hour, seen from a bridge, with station architecture, overhead wires, warm windows, muted colour and subtle grain; wide editorial composition; no readable signs or text.
- `berlin-stairs.webp`: Documentary architectural photograph of a Berlin U-Bahn entrance and tiled corridor after rain, with a single passerby carrying an umbrella, stone surfaces, warm light and muted tones; portrait editorial composition; no readable signs or text.
- `tempelhof.webp`: Quiet documentary photograph of the open Tempelhof field on a hazy autumn morning, a long paved path with distant cyclists, pale sky and restrained colours; portrait composition; no text.
- `stockholm.webp`: Documentary travel photograph of Stockholm's waterfront in overcast late-summer light, layered older buildings and a small ferry, seen across the water with muted colour and natural grain; wide composition; no readable signage or text.

Replace these previews and remove this folder once the site is filled with your own work.
